Three interactive dashboards were developed to analyze restaurant performance from
strategic, operational, and market perspectives.

### 1) Executive Performance Overview
This dashboard presents a high-level overview of platform performance. Key KPIs such as
average rating (3.996), total restaurants (7,432), average price (Rs 305), and percentage
of rated restaurants (75.3%) establish the overall market baseline. Cuisine-level analysis
indicates that North Indian and Beverages categories perform strongly, while city-level
comparison shows Mumbai and Bangalore leading in ratings. The dashboard supports
quick executive decision-making by summarizing quality, pricing, and market size.

### 2) Operational Drill-Down
This dashboard provides detailed insights into relationships between pricing, demand,
and quality. The price vs rating scatter shows a weak negative correlation, indicating
that higher prices do not necessarily lead to better ratings. City demand analysis high-
lights Bangalore as the highest demand market, followed by Hyderabad, while Kolkata
and Mumbai show comparatively lower demand. The cuisine-city heatmap reveals per-
formance gaps across segments, identifying potential areas for strategic expansion.

### 3) Market Insights Dashboard
This dashboard focuses on value positioning, customer preferences, and supply-quality
relationships. The value index highlights cuisines such as Snacks and Beverages as of-
fering strong value for money. The offers vs rating analysis shows minimal impact of
discounts on customer satisfaction. Additionally, the supply vs quality scatter demon-
strates that higher restaurant density does not always result in better ratings. The veg
vs city comparison indicates stronger performance of vegetarian restaurants in selected
markets.

Overall, the dashboard suite enables multi-level analysis by combining strategic overview,
operational insights, and market-level patterns to support data-driven decision making.
